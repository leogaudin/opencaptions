# Add animated captions to a video on your iPhone, with no upload

OpenCaptions adds animated, word-by-word captions to a video on your iPhone, iPad or Mac. The speech model runs on
the device, so the video is never uploaded, there is no account, and it works without a connection once a model is
downloaded. It needs iOS 18 or later.

## In five steps

1. **Add a video** from Photos with **+**. You can also send one from Files, from another app's share sheet, or with
   the **Caption a Video** Shortcuts action.
2. **Transcribe.** Choose a speech model and the language. The first use of a model downloads it (about 220 MB for the
   small ones, about 630 MB for the big ones), and the first transcription on a device is slower while the model is
   optimized for it. After that it loads in seconds.
3. **Fix the words.** Double-tap a word to correct it. Names and jargon are the usual misses, and each speech model
   makes a few.
4. **Pick a look.** The free app has Bold, Purple Punch, Karaoke, Paper and Plain, and Pro adds the other fourteen.
   Drag the caption to move it, and pinch to change its size.
5. **Save.** Choose the size and frame rate. The video is saved as H.264, or as 10-bit HEVC when the original is HDR,
   and HDR stays HDR.

Your work is saved by itself as you go, so you can leave and come back.

## What is free

The free app has everything needed to caption a video and save it up to 1080p at 30 frames per second, with a small mark
in the corner. **Pro** is one purchase, with no subscription: it removes the mark and adds 4K, 60 fps, HDR and every
caption style. One purchase covers the iPhone, the iPad and the Mac. All the code is open source, and a build you make
yourself has everything unlocked.

## Which speech model

| Choose | When |
|---|---|
| **Parakeet Ultra** (the default) | Fast, for 25 European languages. |
| **Whisper Large v3** | The best all-round accuracy, for about 100 languages. |
| **Apple Speech** | Nothing to download, on iOS 26 and later. |

The [speech model benchmarks](../project/speech-model-benchmarks.md) show how they compare in 13 languages.

## Limits

There is no Android app. The app transcribes speech and styles it, with no translation, and no automatic emoji or
B-roll. If you need those, a hosted tool is the better fit: see the
[comparison](./caption-app-alternatives.md).
