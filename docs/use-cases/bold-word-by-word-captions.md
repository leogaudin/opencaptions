# Bold word-by-word captions, free and open source

Short-form video has a house style: heavy capitals, a few words at a time, the word being said lit up in a
colour. People often search for it as "Hormozi-style" captions. OpenCaptions makes that look from your own video, in
a few steps, with an open-source editor you can run yourself.

## How to make them

1. Add your video and transcribe it. Every word gets its own timing.
2. Pick a look. **Bold** is heavy type with each word popping as it is said. **Purple Punch** slides a coloured box
   from word to word. **Karaoke** sweeps colour across each word. **Boom** bounces big words in a display font.
3. Set how many words show at once, and move the caption with your finger or the mouse.
4. Fix any misheard word on the video, then save.

Every look is a starting point: the [style panel](../guides/styling.md) changes the font, the colours, the outline, the
animation and the timing.

## What it costs

The web editor is free to self-host, with every look. In the [iPhone app](../guides/iphone-app.md#free-and-pro) Bold,
Purple Punch, Karaoke, Paper and Plain are free, up to 1080p at 30 fps with a small mark. Pro is one purchase, with
no subscription.

## Questions

**Does it work in my language?**
Yes, in about 100 spoken languages with Whisper, and captions draw in Latin, Cyrillic, Arabic, Hebrew, Indic, Thai and
CJK scripts.

**Is my video uploaded?**
No. On the iPhone it is transcribed on the device, and on your own server it stays on that server.

**Can I try it first?**
Yes, [in your browser](https://opencaptions.app), with nothing to install.
