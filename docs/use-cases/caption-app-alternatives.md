# Alternatives to Submagic, Captions, Zeemo and CapCut

Submagic, Captions, Zeemo and CapCut all add animated captions to short videos. OpenCaptions does the same job and
differs in how it is paid for and where the work happens: it is open source, the app transcribes on your device, and
Pro is one purchase. We make OpenCaptions, so read this with that in mind. The rows for the other tools are only what
each one's own pricing page says.

## What the pricing pages say

From each tool's pricing page on 24 November 2026. Prices and limits change, so check before you decide.

| | Pricing | Free | Cheapest paid, as listed | Limits listed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Submagic** | Subscription, monthly or yearly, plus credit packs | No free plan on its pricing page | Starter: 19 € a month, or 12 € a month billed 144 € a year | Starter: videos up to 2 minutes, 1080p at 30 fps |
| **Captions** | Subscription, monthly or yearly, plus AI credits | A limited free version | Max: $24.99 a month (iOS prices) | Captions in 100+ languages |
| **Zeemo** | Subscription of credits | Yes: 120 credits a year, videos up to 1 minute or 200 MB, 720p exports | Pro: US$8 a month | Captions in 95 languages, translation in 124+ |
| **CapCut** | Subscription (Free, Pro, Ultra, Teams) | A free plan, and a 7-day Pro trial | Pro: 29.99 € a month, or 259.99 € a year | Not stated for auto captions |
| **OpenCaptions** | Free app, and one purchase for Pro | Yes: five looks, up to 1080p at 30 fps, a small mark | One purchase, no subscription: the price is on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6820548922) | On the device: 25 European languages with Parakeet Ultra, about 100 with Whisper Large v3 |

## Where OpenCaptions is different

- **Your video stays put.** On iPhone, iPad and Mac the speech model runs on the device, and nothing is uploaded. The
  server version runs on hardware you look after.
- **No subscription.** A single purchase unlocks Pro, and the code is open source (AGPL-3.0): a build from source has
  everything unlocked.
- **The preview is the export.** One engine draws what you see and what you save, so there are no surprises when the
  video is saved.

## Where they are ahead

- **Translation.** OpenCaptions transcribes in the spoken language and does not translate, though you can
  [import subtitles](../guides/editing.md#edit-as-text-and-import-subtitles) translated elsewhere. Zeemo's pricing
  page lists translation in 124+ languages.
- **Languages.** Captions lists captions in 100+ languages and Zeemo 95. OpenCaptions covers about 100 with Whisper,
  and 25 European languages with the fastest model.
- **Android.** Zeemo lists apps for the App Store and Google Play. OpenCaptions has no Android app.
- **Automation.** OpenCaptions has no automatic emoji, B-roll or editing, and it has fewer templates than the tools
  that sell them.
- **A full editor.** CapCut is a complete video editor, with captions as one feature. OpenCaptions only makes
  captions, so you edit the video elsewhere.

## Which to choose

Choose OpenCaptions if you caption videos now and then and would rather pay once, if the footage should stay on your
phone, or if you want to run or change the software yourself. Choose a hosted tool if you want the most templates,
translation, or an Android app.
