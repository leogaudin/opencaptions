# Support

**Email [support@opencaptions.app](mailto:support@opencaptions.app)** with what you were doing and what happened.
For the app, the diagnostics from Settings help: they stay on your phone until you choose to send them.

**A bug or an idea?** [Open an issue](https://github.com/leogaudin/opencaptions/issues) on GitHub, where others can
find the answer too. Running your own server? Look at [troubleshooting](./self-hosting/troubleshooting.md) first.

**A purchase?** Apple handles payments and refunds, at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
To get Pro back on a new device, open **OpenCaptions Pro** in Settings and choose **Restore purchases**.

Many questions are answered in the [FAQ](./faq.md).
